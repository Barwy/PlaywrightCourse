2 types of dialogue boxes:
- 'regular ones' where we click and box appears that can be investigated with devtools.
- ones that are built into the browser.
The second type is automatically dismissed by Playwright. To validate the message and click OK:
page.on('dialog', dialog => {
    expect(dialog.message()).toEqual('Message text');
    dialog.accept();
})

page.on must be declared before the event in which it appears, ot it won't work.
