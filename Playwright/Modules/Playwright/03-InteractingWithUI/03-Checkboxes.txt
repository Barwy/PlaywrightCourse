There are two ways to deal with checkboxes:
1. .click() - clicks the checkbox.
2. .check() - ensures the checkbox is left in checked state (nothing happens if it is already checked).

The opposite of .check() is .uncheck();

await page.getByRole('checkbox', {name: 'Hide on click'}).click({force: true});
await page.getByRole('checkbox', {name: 'Hide on click'}).check({force: true});

(un)Checking all boxes on a page:
const allCheckboxes = page.getByRole('checkbox');
for(const box of await allCheckboxes.all()){
    await box.uncheck({force: true});
    await expect(box).not.toBeChecked();
}
