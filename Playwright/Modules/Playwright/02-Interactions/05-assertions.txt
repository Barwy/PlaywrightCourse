Thee are two types of assertions. To see what type we are using, we need to hover over the method we use to assert a value,
we will be sown its signature. Generis assertions use GenericAssertions.
They are simple. They compare their right side to the left:

let number = 3;
expect(value).toEqual(3);

There is a variety of methods to choose from.

We can also perform assertions on locators. Good thing about them is that they wait 5s for text to appear. 
They have LocatorAssertions in their descriptions when hovered over:
const elementLocator = page.locator('locator);
await expect(elementLocator).toHaveText('Text');

Finally, there are alos soft asseertins that let the test continue if it fails:
const elementLocator = page.locator('locator);
await expect.soft(elementLocator).toHaveText('Text');
